# Prompts de ilustração — Esperando Heitor

Todas as peças que desenhei em SVG, descritas como prompt para gerar em outra IA
e comparar.

**Como usar:** cole o **Bloco de estilo** no início de *todo* prompt, depois o
prompt específico da peça, depois o **Bloco técnico** no fim. O bloco de estilo é
o que mantém as 27 peças parecendo do mesmo desenhista — sem ele, cada imagem
volta com um traço diferente e o conjunto desmonta.

**Ordem importa:** gere primeiro a peça `00` (folha de personagem). Depois use a
imagem resultante como *referência visual* em todas as poses do bebê. O mesmo
vale para os animais. Sem referência, o bebê da pose 3 não é o mesmo bebê da
pose 1, e a animação denuncia.

---

## Bloco de estilo (prepend em todos)

```
Delicate children's-book watercolour illustration with a fine ink contour line.
Soft granulated watercolour washes with visible pigment texture and slightly
irregular, hand-painted edges — the paint does not fill the outline perfectly.
Thin, confident brown-ink line (never black), varying subtly in weight.
Refined, editorial, understated — the register of a premium nursery brand or a
Scandinavian children's book, NOT a cartoon, NOT clip-art, NOT a sticker.
Calm and tender rather than cute-aggressive.

Colour palette, strictly:
  warm off-white  #FBF7F0
  paper cream     #F5EFE4
  sand            #F0D8B6
  taupe           #B3926F
  bark brown      #8A6A4D
  ink brown       #4A3728
  baby blue       #D1E2F3
  dusty blue      #7BA5C6
  deep navy       #032A42
  skin            #F2D8C2
Nothing saturated, no neon, no pink, no rainbow.

Flat lighting, no cast shadow on the ground, no gradient background.
```

## Bloco técnico (append em todos)

```
Transparent background (PNG with alpha), fully cut out, no backdrop, no frame,
no ground shadow, no vignette.
Subject centred with ~12% empty margin on all sides.
Square canvas 2048x2048 unless stated otherwise.
No text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no signature.
Single subject only, no duplicates, no collage, no variations grid.
```

## Prompt negativo (se a ferramenta aceitar)

```
photorealistic, 3d render, cgi, plastic, glossy, vector flat design, clip art,
sticker with white outline, thick black outline, heavy drop shadow, neon,
saturated colours, pink, rainbow, glitter, confetti, text, letters, watermark,
busy background, pattern background, multiple characters, grid of variations,
creepy face, uncanny realistic baby, big anime eyes
```

# 1 — Heitor, o bebê

O personagem que interage com os produtos no catálogo e guarda o presente na
caixa. Precisa ser **o mesmo bebê** nas seis poses.

### 00 · Folha de personagem *(gere esta primeiro)*

```
Character reference sheet of one baby, three views side by side on the same
baseline: front view, three-quarter view, side view. Standing/seated neutral pose.

The baby: roughly one year old, chubby round head noticeably larger than the
body (about 1 head to 1.6 bodies), soft round cheeks with a faint warm blush,
two small dark oval eyes with a single tiny highlight, no eyebrows, a small
closed smile. One single curl of light brown hair on top, drawn as one looping
ink stroke. Small round ears. Wearing a plain pale-blue sleeveless romper with a
white collar line and two tiny white buttons down the front. Bare arms and legs,
simple rounded hands and feet without individual fingers.

Canvas 2048x1024, horizontal.
```

### 01 · Sentado

```
The baby from the reference, seated on the floor facing the viewer, legs
splayed forward and slightly apart, both arms relaxed down at his sides with the
hands resting on the ground. Calm, content expression. Symmetrical, upright.
```

*Onde entra:* ao lado do produto, no card do catálogo.

### 02 · Espiando por cima de uma borda

```
The baby from the reference seen only from the chest up, as if peeking over the
top edge of a wall: just the head and the two hands gripping an invisible
horizontal ledge, the fingertips curled over it. The body below the hands is not
drawn — the illustration ends cleanly at that line. Curious, wide-eyed
expression looking straight at the viewer.

Canvas 2048x1400, horizontal. Leave the bottom edge of the drawing flat and
open, with no margin at the bottom.
```

*Onde entra:* aparece por cima da borda do card quando ele entra na tela.

### 03 · Acenando

```
The baby from the reference, seated as in pose 01, but his left arm (viewer's
right) is raised up beside his head, elbow slightly bent, open hand up in a
small wave. The other arm stays relaxed at his side. Happy expression.
```

*Onde entra:* perto dos botões principais. O braço é animado separadamente, então
**desenhe o braço levantado bem destacado do corpo**, sem encostar na cabeça.

### 04 · Segurando uma etiqueta

```
The baby from the reference, seated, holding up in front of his chest with both
hands a small blank rectangular paper tag (a luggage-label shape, cream coloured,
thin ink border, slightly tilted). The tag covers his torso but not his face.
The tag is completely blank — no writing on it.
```

*Onde entra:* card de preço. O texto entra por cima, em HTML.

### 05 · Dormindo

```
The baby from the reference, lying asleep on his side on an invisible surface,
head to the right, knees drawn up towards the belly, one arm tucked under and
one arm resting along the body. Eyes closed, drawn as two simple upward-curving
ink arcs. Peaceful.

Canvas 2048x1400, horizontal.
```

*Onde entra:* no fim do site, na mensagem final em azul-noite.

### 06 · Guardando — braços levantados

```
The baby from the reference, seated, both arms stretched straight up above his
shoulders, open hands raised, as if placing something into a container above
him. Head tilted slightly back, looking up. The two raised arms are well clear
of the head silhouette on both sides.
```

*Onde entra:* é ele quem coloca o envelope e o presente dentro da caixa.

# 2 — Os animais

Três moradores, e só três: girafa, passarinho e ursinho. Aparecem poucas vezes
cada um.

### 07 · Girafa inteira

```
A young giraffe standing in profile facing right, drawn in the same delicate
watercolour style. Sand-coloured body #F0D8B6 with soft irregular taupe #C9A074
patches, a long slender neck, a short bark-brown mane along the neck, two small
ossicones with rounded tips on the head, a small leaf-shaped ear, one visible
dark eye with a single lash stroke, four slim legs with darker hooves, a thin
tail ending in a small tuft. Gentle, friendly, slightly sleepy expression. Not a
cartoon giraffe with a big grin.

Canvas 1600x2048, vertical.
```

### 08 · Girafa espiando *(recorte)*

```
Only the head and the upper part of the neck of the same giraffe, entering the
frame from the bottom edge, as if the animal were looking over the edge of
something. Head turned three-quarters towards the viewer. The neck is cut off
flat by the bottom edge of the canvas with no margin below.

Canvas 2048x1400, horizontal.
```

### 09 · Passarinho pousado

```
A very small round bird perched, seen in profile facing right. Baby-blue #D1E2F3
body with a dusty-blue #7BA5C6 wing, a tiny warm-ochre triangular beak, one dark
dot eye, two thin tail feathers, two hair-thin legs. Extremely simple — five or
six shapes in total. Sweet, not detailed.

Canvas 2048x1600, horizontal.
```

### 10 · Passarinho voando

```
The same small bird in flight, seen in profile facing right, both wings open and
raised, legs tucked. Same simplicity.

Canvas 2048x1600, horizontal.
```

### 11 · Ursinho sentado

```
A small teddy-bear-like bear cub seated facing the viewer, in the same delicate
watercolour style. Warm sand-and-taupe fur (#F0D8B6 body with #B3926F muzzle,
inner ears and paw pads), round head with two small round ears, a soft oval
muzzle with a tiny dark nose and a small closed smile, two dark dot eyes, short
round arms and legs. Calm and gentle, like a well-loved cloth toy — not a
realistic bear and not a grinning cartoon.

Canvas 2048x2048.
```

### 12 · Ursinho espiando *(recorte)*

```
Only the head and both front paws of the same bear cub, peeking over an
invisible horizontal ledge: head and paws visible, body below the paws not
drawn, the illustration ending flat at that line. Looking straight at the viewer.

Canvas 2048x1400, horizontal. No margin at the bottom.
```

# 3 — As miudezas

Pequenas, poucas, espalhadas com parcimônia.

### 13 · Balão

```
A single small balloon, teardrop shape, in soft baby blue #D1E2F3 with one pale
highlight stroke, a small triangular knot at the bottom and a thin curling
bark-brown string hanging down. Nothing written on it.

Canvas 1200x2048, vertical.
```

*Gere duas vezes: uma em azul bebê `#D1E2F3`, outra em areia `#F0D8B6`.*

### 14 · Folha

```
A single simple leaf, pointed oval shape, soft sage green with a thin darker

Canvas 1200x2048, vertical.
```

### 15 · Faísca

```
A single four-pointed sparkle / star-shaped glint with slightly concave sides,

Canvas 1024x1024.
```

### 16 · Coração do lacre

```
A single heart shaped like a drop of red sealing wax pressed onto paper: soft
deep red #B04A4A, slightly irregular hand-painted edge, subtly domed, no shine,

Canvas 1024x1024.
```

### 17 · Pegadas de bebê

```
Four small bare baby footprints in soft taupe #B3926F, arranged as a walking

Canvas 2048x900, horizontal.
```

### 18 · Nuvem

Canvas 2048x900, horizontal.

# 4 — A cerimônia do envio

**Estas são as peças mais delicadas do conjunto.** A animação move cada uma
separadamente, então elas precisam vir em arquivos separados, na mesma
perspectiva e na mesma escala. Se a caixa vier como uma imagem só, nada pode
entrar dentro dela.

### 19 · Folha de papel de carta

```
A single blank sheet of writing paper seen flat from directly above, portrait
orientation, warm off-white #FDFAF4 with a very subtle paper texture and softly
irregular hand-torn edges. Completely blank — no lines, no ruling, no text, no
border, no decoration. Very subtle soft shadow beneath it is acceptable.

Canvas 1500x2048, vertical.
```

### 20 · Papel dobrado em três

```
The same sheet of writing paper, now folded into three horizontal panels
(letter-fold), seen flat from directly above. Two soft horizontal crease lines
visible, the folded edges slightly raised. Blank.

Canvas 2048x900, horizontal.
```

### 21 · Envelope fechado

```
The front of a closed envelope seen flat from directly above, landscape
orientation, warm off-white #FDFAF4 paper with a thin taupe #B3926F contour
line. The triangular back flap is folded down over the front, its diagonal edges
visible, meeting at a point in the middle. Two faint diagonal seam lines from
the lower corners. No address, no stamp, no writing, no seal.

Canvas 2048x1400, horizontal.
```

### 22 · Envelope com a aba aberta

```
The same envelope, same size, same angle, but with the triangular flap open and
folded upwards above the envelope body, revealing the opening. Exactly the same
envelope proportions and colours as the previous piece.

Canvas 2048x1800, horizontal.
```

### 23 · Caixa — camada FUNDO

```
Only the inner back wall and the open mouth of a gift box, seen straight on from
slightly above: a dark warm-brown rectangular opening (#6A4F38 at the far edge
fading to #8A6A4D), reading as the shadowed inside of an open box. No front
wall, no lid, no ribbon. This is the layer that sits BEHIND whatever is placed
inside the box.

Canvas 2048x1730, horizontal. Keep the box centred and note the exact position —
pieces 23, 24 and 25 must align perfectly when stacked.
```

### 24 · Caixa — camada FRENTE

```
Only the front wall of the same gift box: a sand-coloured #F0D8B6 panel, very
slightly wider at the top than at the bottom, with soft light falling from the
left, and a vertical dusty-blue #7BA5C6 ribbon running down the centre. No lid,
no top face, no inside. Flat top edge.

Canvas 2048x1730, horizontal. Same canvas and alignment as piece 23.
```

### 25 · Caixa — camada TAMPA

```
Only the lid of the same gift box, seen straight on from slightly above: a
shallow sand-coloured #ECD0A8 lid with a lighter top face, a dusty-blue #7BA5C6
ribbon band across the centre, and a soft simple two-loop bow on top in the same
blue. Nothing below the lid.

Canvas 2048x1730, horizontal. Same canvas and alignment as pieces 23 and 24, with
the lid positioned as it would sit closed on the box.
```

# 5 — Texturas

### 26 · Mancha de aquarela

```
A single soft abstract watercolour wash blot, warm sand #F0D8B6, with granulated
pigment texture and irregular bleeding edges, fading to nothing at the borders.
No shape, no subject — just pigment on wet paper.

Canvas 2048x2048.
```

### 27 · Grão de papel

```
A seamless tileable texture of fine cold-press watercolour paper grain, warm
off-white, very subtle, no pattern, no fibres visible as lines. Neutral and
extremely low contrast.

Canvas 1024x1024, seamless tile.
```

**Duas coisas para checar antes de aprovar cada imagem:**

1. **Fundo transparente de verdade.** Muita ferramenta entrega um fundo branco
   que *parece* recortado. Abra num visualizador com fundo xadrez. Se vier
   branco, o desenho vai aparecer com um retângulo em volta em cima da areia.
2. **É o mesmo bebê?** Coloque as seis poses lado a lado. Se o cabelo, o
   macacão ou o formato do rosto mudarem entre elas, a animação vai parecer que
   trocaram de criança no meio.
